Part 0The intent

Sublimating the immediate motivation into infrastructure of broader use

Formalized evidence for one proof: the spin-representation roadmap targets.

Infrastructure of broader use

Give back to the tools the project relied on: TauCeti refactors first, Mathlib where it earns it.

“Contributing to large, established open source projects in general, and to mathlib in particular, is indeed a skill that needs to be acquired. It does require sublimating the immediate motivation … into longer-term building of infrastructure of broader use …, working together with the community to find the best way to formalize each element used in the proof in suitable generality …” — Joseph Myers, Lean Zulip · FLT stream, “Main blockers for a full proof of FLT.”, message 614609978 (via the session brief of 2026-08-05)

The CliffordAlgebra work has two deliverables in parallel. The immediate one is the spin-representation roadmap. The second is the reusable infrastructure the work generates — inside TauCeti first, upstream in Mathlib where it earns it. This report walks that second deliverable end to end: the landscape (who is attacking which layer), the patterns (what repeats in the proofs), the proposals (one reusable piece per pattern, each checked for mathematical soundness and TauCetiReview-rubric compliance), the grounding (which proposals the roadmap already names, and the surgical edits that anchor the rest), and the route forward (which angles are reasonable, and how to learn the reuse lesson earlier).

1First-use extraction (rule of two). Ship the criterion lemmas with the PR that first repeats the shape: filtration_comap_le_iff with #1794, the exterior-power range criterion with #1708, the named quotient relation with #1903. Later slices consume; reviewers re-litigate nothing.
2Upstream-first for structural API. The generic associated-graded construction (P5) and the exterior-power criterion (P3) are Mathlib-shaped. Writing the Mathlib PR first would have made #1903/#1971 thin specializations instead of the boilerplate the reuse rubric then peeled (P5/P6/P11/P12/P14/P15). The roadmap already authorizes upstreaming; do it before the specialization, not after.
3Design reconnaissance as a hard gate. The three dead ends were each preventable by the compact design check — search by structure, consumer probe first, natural generality, roadmap-README-first. This is the operational form of the quote’s “sublimate the immediate motivation”.
4A shared reuse registry per layer. PR claims already list files; extend them to list the reusable declarations each slice adds, so parallel workers on one layer reuse instead of re-proving — the coordination side of “working together with the community”.
5Split the PBW milestone: construction → specialization → isomorphism. Pin filtrationGradedEquivExterior (the Suggested.lean edit) and route it through the generic construction, so the milestone’s own proof is a specialization and the total isomorphism is what the next slice claims.

Each item starts with the consumer probe written before the source, per the review-gate retrospective. As of 2026-08-08 the operator selected the canonical Layer 3 route (consume #2224, land #2335, then the quadratic Lie action) and the Layer 9 CAR lane, not the Layer 2/4 angles below; the merged PBW sequence (#1847/#1903/#1971) landed the proposed P2/P1 surfaces, and P5 (Mathlib) and P3 remain open. The layer-2 double cover and the Layer 4 spin module remain the next angles after the current lanes; the module-making (wedge plus contraction, spinAction) is expected to generate its own reusable criteria, which the rule-of-two and the registry are meant to capture on first use.
